Assignment name  : pgcd
Expected files   : pgcd.c
Allowed functions: printf, atoi, malloc, free
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that receives two strings, each representing a strictly
positive integer that fits in an int.

Print their greatest common divisor followed by a newline (the result is
always a strictly positive integer).

If the number of arguments is not 2, print only a newline.

Examples:

$> ./pgcd 42 10 | cat -e
2$
$> ./pgcd 42 12 | cat -e
6$
$> ./pgcd 14 77 | cat -e
7$
$> ./pgcd 17 3 | cat -e
1$
$> ./pgcd | cat -e
$
