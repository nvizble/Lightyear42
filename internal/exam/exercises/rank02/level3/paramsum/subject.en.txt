Assignment name  : paramsum
Expected files   : paramsum.c
Allowed functions: write
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that prints how many arguments it received, followed by a
newline.

When it receives no arguments, it prints 0 followed by a newline.

Example:

$>./paramsum 1 2 3 5 7 24
6
$>./paramsum 6 12 24 | cat -e
3$
$>./paramsum | cat -e
0$
$>
