Assignment name  : lcm
Expected files   : lcm.c
Allowed functions: none
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a function that receives two unsigned ints and returns their LCM.

The LCM (Lowest Common Multiple) of two non-zero integers is the smallest
positive integer that both of them divide.

There are two usual ways to compute it:

- Walk through the multiples of each integer until you reach a common
multiple other than 0.

- Use the HCF (Highest Common Factor) of the two integers:

	LCM(x, y) = | x * y | / HCF(x, y)

  where | x * y | is the absolute value of the product of x and y.

If either integer is 0, the LCM is 0.

Your function must be prototyped as follows:

  unsigned int    lcm(unsigned int a, unsigned int b);

Turn in the function only, without a main.
