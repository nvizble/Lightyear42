Assignment name  : add_prime_sum
Expected files   : add_prime_sum.c
Allowed functions: write, exit
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that receives a positive integer and prints the sum of every
prime number less than or equal to it, followed by a newline.

When the program does not receive exactly 1 argument, or when the argument is
not a positive number, it prints only 0 followed by a newline.

The examples below are correct.

Examples:

$>./add_prime_sum 5
10
$>./add_prime_sum 7 | cat -e
17$
$>./add_prime_sum | cat -e
0$
$>
