Assignment name  : fprime
Expected files   : fprime.c
Allowed functions: printf, atoi
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that receives a positive int and prints its prime factors on
the standard output, followed by a newline.

The factors must appear in ascending order, separated by '*', so that the
printed expression evaluates to the given number.

If the number of parameters is not 1, just print a newline.

When a parameter is given, it will always be valid.

Examples:

$> ./fprime 225225 | cat -e
3*3*5*5*7*11*13$
$> ./fprime 8333325 | cat -e
3*3*5*5*7*11*13*37$
$> ./fprime 9539 | cat -e
9539$
$> ./fprime 804577 | cat -e
804577$
$> ./fprime 42 | cat -e
2*3*7$
$> ./fprime 1 | cat -e
1$
$> ./fprime | cat -e
$
$> ./fprime 42 21 | cat -e
$
