Assignment name  : fizzbuzz
Expected files   : fizzbuzz.c
Allowed functions: write
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that writes every number from 1 to 100, one per line (each
followed by a newline).

Numbers that are multiples of 3 are written as 'fizz' instead.

Numbers that are multiples of 5 are written as 'buzz' instead.

Numbers that are multiples of both 3 and 5 are written as 'fizzbuzz' instead.

Example:

$>./fizzbuzz
1
2
fizz
4
buzz
fizz
7
8
fizz
buzz
11
fizz
13
14
fizzbuzz
[...]
97
98
fizz
buzz
$>
