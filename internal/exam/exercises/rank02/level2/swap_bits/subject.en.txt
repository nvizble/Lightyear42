Assignment name  : swap_bits
Expected files   : swap_bits.c
Allowed functions: none
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a function that takes a byte, exchanges its two halves (its upper and
lower 4 bits, as shown below) and returns the result.

It must be prototyped as follows:

unsigned char	swap_bits(unsigned char octet);

Example:

  1 byte
_____________
 0100 | 0001
     \ /
     / \
 0001 | 0100

Turn in the function only, without a main.
