Assignment name  : alpha_mirror
Expected files   : alpha_mirror.c
Allowed functions: write
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program named alpha_mirror that takes a string and prints it with
every letter swapped for its mirror in the alphabet, followed by a newline.

The mirror of a letter is the one at the same distance from the other end
of the alphabet:

'a' becomes 'z', 'Z' becomes 'A'
'd' becomes 'w', 'M' becomes 'N'

and so on.

The case of each letter is kept. Characters that are not letters are printed
unchanged.

If the number of arguments is not 1, print only a newline.

Examples:

$>./alpha_mirror "abc"
zyx
$>./alpha_mirror "My horse is Amazing." | cat -e
Nb slihv rh Znzarmt.$
$>./alpha_mirror | cat -e
$
$>
