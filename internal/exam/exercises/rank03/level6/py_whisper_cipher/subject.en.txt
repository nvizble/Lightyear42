Write a function that encrypts a string with a Caesar cipher.

def whisper_cipher(text: str, shift: int) -> str:

Every letter from 'a' to 'z' and from 'A' to 'Z' is replaced by the
letter shift positions after it in the alphabet, wrapping around from
'z' back to 'a' (and 'Z' to 'A'). Letters keep their case. A negative
shift moves the letters backwards, and the shift can be any integer,
even larger than 26 in either direction. Every other character (digits,
spaces, punctuation, accented letters like 'é') is copied unchanged.

Examples:

whisper_cipher("hello", 3)          -> "khoor"
whisper_cipher("Hello World!", 1)   -> "Ifmmp Xpsme!"
whisper_cipher("xyz", 3)            -> "abc"
whisper_cipher("ABC123def", 5)      -> "FGH123ijk"
whisper_cipher("abc", -3)           -> "xyz"
whisper_cipher("abc", 52)           -> "abc"
whisper_cipher("café", 1)           -> "dbgé"
whisper_cipher("", 10)              -> ""
