Write a function that alternates the case of the letters of a string.

def string_sculptor(text: str) -> str:

A letter is a character for which str.isalpha() is True. Inside each
word, the 1st letter becomes lowercase, the 2nd uppercase, the 3rd
lowercase, and so on. Characters that are not letters are copied
unchanged and do not count in the alternation (the letter right after
them takes the next case in the sequence). A space (' ') restarts the
alternation: the first letter after a space is lowercase again. Other
characters such as '-', ',' or '.' do not restart it.

Examples:

string_sculptor("hello")         -> "hElLo"
string_sculptor("Hello World")   -> "hElLo wOrLd"
string_sculptor("abc123def")     -> "aBc123DeF"
string_sculptor("Python3.9!")    -> "pYtHoN3.9!"
string_sculptor("hello,world")   -> "hElLo,WoRlD"
string_sculptor("ab cd ef")      -> "aB cD eF"
string_sculptor("")              -> ""
